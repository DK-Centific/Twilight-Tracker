Twilight Tracker · For admins

Incident Report

When a moderator escalates, a high-priority email goes out. The same report lands here for you to read and follow up.

This is the desk side of the panic button. The field sends. You get the email. Then you open the card in Twilight.

The path

1. Field sendsA moderator taps the red shield and Send report (or Call 911). 2. Email landsA high-priority message arrives at the Twilight inbox. 3. You open itThe same report appears under Incident Report.

How the email works

The app writes the email the moment they tap Send. You do not have to forward anything.

HIGH PRIORITY · Twilight escalation · Data Loss · Alex

Goes toben_prod_twilight@centific.com
Marked asHigh priority
SubjectHIGH PRIORITY · Twilight escalation · [type] · [their name]
InsideType, Environment or Teammate (if misconduct), who sent it, team, session date, address, time, their comment, and the Teams reminder when it applies.
Also savedThe same facts are stored as an Incident Report card in Twilight.

For Data Loss and NDA / Consent, the email also says: Also, please notify the managers in the Teams chat immediately.

The email is the alert. It does not replace a call to the project manager, or a message in Teams.

Open Incident Report

Sign in as an Admin. Use either path:

Faster

Tap the red Incident Report pill in the admin bar. You land on the incident list.

From tabs

Tap Performance. Then tap Incident Report (next to Sessions).

Find the right card

The list uses the same four icons the field team taps.

Police ReportDanger or police on site. Data LossA capture is gone or will not open. QuestionsNDA or consent they could not answer. MisconductHarm, a threat, or an allegation.
Tap a tile at the top
All · Police Report · Data Loss · Questions · Misconduct
Search or pick dates
Search a name, team, or comment. Dates: Today, This week, This month, All time, or Custom.
Tap a card
Or tap its Incident Report pill. You will see type, who sent it, team, session, time, assignment, email status, and the comment.
Export Excel
Tap Export Excel. The file uses the filters on screen. Name starts with Twilight_Incidents_.

What you do next

Same night

Open the email and the card. Reply in Teams. Confirm the project manager knows. Keep the card as the record.

If email says failed

The card can still be here. Open Incident Report and read the comment. Ask the moderator to send again if the facts are missing.
A new report shows right away on the computer or phone that sent it. Other admins see shared reports once they appear in the common log.
