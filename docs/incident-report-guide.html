Handle an Incident Report

A walkthrough for the person watching the inbox tonight. Same clicks you will make in Twilight.

A moderator just tapped Send report (or Call 911). Two things happen at the same time: a high-priority email lands, and a card appears for you in Twilight. This page is what you do next.

You are the admin. Your job is to see it, read it, tell the project manager, and follow up in Teams the same night.

Step 1 · Sign in

  1. Open Twilight Tracker.
  2. Type your Orbit login. Admins on the passwordless list (for example Admin-Twilight) do not type a password.
  3. Click Sign In.

You should see the Project Twilight sign-in card. Here the username is Admin-Twilight.

Step 2 · Find the red Incident Report pill

After sign-in you land on the console. Across the tabs, on the right, is a red pill that says Incident Report. That is the fastest way in.

Look at the tab row: Overview, Moderator Hub, Performance, Approval, then the yellow Activities pill, then the red Incident Report pill.

The red one with the warning triangle is Incident Report. Tap that.

Step 3 · See tonight’s cards

You should now be on Incident Report. Each card is one send from the field. The number next to the tab is how many cards are stored.

This is the real Incident Report screen. Example night: one Data Loss, one Contact Police, one Misconduct.

On a quiet night the list can be empty. That is fine. When a moderator sends, a card shows up here.

Step 4 · Narrow the list

If more than a couple of cards are in, tap a type at the top. The number on each tile is the count.

All / Police Report / Data Loss / Questions / Misconduct. Questions is the NDA or consent send.

Then use search or dates the same way you do on Sessions.

  1. Type a name, team, or word from the comment in the search box.
  2. Tap Today if you only want tonight.
  3. Or tap This week, This month, All time, or Custom.
  4. When you need a file, tap Export Excel. It uses whatever is on screen.

Search on the left. Dates in the middle. Export Excel on the right.

Step 5 · Open the card and read it

Tap the card (or tap the small red Incident Report on that row). It opens. Read the comment first — that is what they typed in the field.

A Data Loss card from Alex Chen, Team 01. Email shows ben_prod_twilight@centific.com · sent. The brown bar is the Teams reminder.

On the card, check these in order:

  1. Comment — what happened, in their words.
  2. Type — Data Loss, Contact Police, Questions, or Misconduct.
  3. Detail — Environment or Teammate, if it is misconduct.
  4. Reported by / Team / Session — who and where.
  5. Email — should say the inbox and sent. If it says failed, still use this card. The facts are here even if the mail did not go out.
If the brown bar is there, message managers in Teams now. Then tell the project manager.

Step 6 · The other way in (from Performance)

You can also get here without the red pill:

  1. Tap Performance.
  2. Tap Incident Report next to Sessions. The number is the card count.

Sessions | Incident Report. The 3 means three cards.

Step 7 · How the email works

You do not forward anything. Twilight writes the email the moment they tap Send or Call 911.

HIGH PRIORITY · Twilight escalation · Data Loss · Alex Chen

Goes toben_prod_twilight@centific.com
Marked asHigh priority — it should sit at the top of the inbox.
SubjectHIGH PRIORITY · Twilight escalation · [type] · [their name]
Same facts as the cardType, who, team, session, address, time, comment, and the Teams reminder when it applies.

Here is the email that matches the Data Loss card above.

This is the real Twilight escalation email. Headline is the type. Pink box is the facts. Follow up is the Teams line.

The email is the tap on the shoulder. The card in Twilight is the record you keep. Neither one replaces a call to the project manager.

Step 8 · Close the loop the same night

  1. Open the email and the matching card.
  2. Reply in the Teams chat if the reminder is on the card.
  3. Tell the project manager.
  4. If you need a copy for someone else, tap Export Excel.
A brand-new send shows right away on the computer or phone that sent it. Other admins see it once it is in the shared log. If your list looks empty and you just got the email, refresh, then check the card on this screen.
