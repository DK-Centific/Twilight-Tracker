Panic button · team guide

Use this when something goes wrong on a session. The red shield button is the panic button. It sits on the right on a computer. On a phone it sits next to Settings.

Twilight Tracker · last updated September 9, 2026 · for Moderators and Admins

In 20 seconds
  1. Tap the red shield.
  2. Tap Emergency.
  3. Tap Escalate immediately (the line that says Press for more options).
  4. Tap one of the four icons.
  5. Type what happened and tap Send report. For police, tap Call 911 after the confirm box.

A high-priority email goes to ben_prod_twilight@centific.com. Admins also see it under Incident Report.

1. The four icons

Icon Name you tap When to use it What happens
Contact Police Police are involved, or someone is in danger. A confirm box asks Call 911 now? Tap OK. Your phone dials 911 and the report is sent.
Data Loss A capture is lost, corrupted, or will not open. Type a comment. The Teams reminder is added for you. Tap Send report.
NDA/Consent Question Someone asks about the consent form and you cannot answer. Type a comment. The Teams reminder is added for you. Tap Send report.
Misconduct Report Someone alleges misconduct, harm, or a threat of harm. Tap Environment misconduct or Teammate misconduct. Type a comment. Tap Send report.
Also notify Ritu and Sander directly for anything in Escalate immediately. The app email does not replace that call or message.

2. Moderator steps

Sign in as a Moderator. The red shield is hidden on the login screen. It appears after you are in.

Open the panic menu

  1. Tap the red shield.
  2. You will see three choices: Comm, Troubleshooting, Emergency.

Comm

  1. Tap Comm.
  2. Teams opens in a new tab so you can message managers.

Troubleshooting

  1. Tap Troubleshooting.
  2. Read the tips (network, night setup, and FAQ).
  3. This does not send email.
  4. Tap the X or tap outside the box to close.

Emergency · pick a tier

  1. Tap Emergency.
  2. Read the four tiers. Only the last one sends a report.
TierUse it forDoes it send a report?
Handle it yourself Normal session problems the scripts already cover. No. This is guidance only.
Flag in real time A neighbor confrontation, police arriving, an unworkable site, a participant who appears intoxicated, inappropriate conduct toward you, or a participant in visible distress. No. Flag it live with your managers.
Report afterward A canceled or cut-short session, a withdrawal request, a complaint, or equipment damage. Same night. No. File the usual same-night report.
Escalate immediately Police contact, lost or corrupted capture, a consent question you cannot answer, misconduct, harm, or threat of harm. Yes. Tap this row, then tap an icon.

Send an Escalate immediately report

  1. On the Emergency screen, tap Escalate immediately (the line that says Press for more options).
  2. Four icons appear. Tap the one that matches.
  3. Follow the matching steps below.

Contact Police

  1. Tap the police icon.
  2. Read: If someone is in danger, call 911 now.
  3. Tap Call 911.
  4. A box asks Call 911 now? Tap OK to dial. Tap Cancel if you are not ready.
  5. After OK, the report is sent. You should see Report sent. and a toast that says Escalation sent.

Data Loss or NDA/Consent Question

  1. Tap Data Loss or NDA/Consent Question.
  2. You will see this reminder already on the form: Also, please notify the managers in the Teams chat immediately.
  3. Type what happened in Comment.
  4. Tap Send report.
  5. Wait until it says Report sent.
  6. Then tell managers in the Teams chat, as the reminder says.

Misconduct Report

  1. Tap Misconduct Report.
  2. Tap 1. Environment misconduct or 2. Teammate misconduct. You must pick one.
  3. Type what happened in Comment.
  4. Tap Send report.
  5. If you skipped the type, you will see: Pick Environment or Teammate misconduct first.

If send fails

  • Could not send. Try again. — tap Send report again.
  • Saved on this device. Email is not set up yet. — the report is stored on that phone or computer. Tell an admin so they can check Incident Report on that same device, or retry after email is working.

Tap Back to return to the four icons. Tap the X to close.

3. What the app fills in for you

You do not type these. The app adds them when you send:

FieldWhere it comes from
Your name and Orbit loginThe account you signed in with
Team name and team numberYour current team
Assignment idThe open session, if you have one
Session dateThe assignment date, or today
AddressThe participant address or team address, if known
Time sentThe moment you tap Send or Call 911

The email subject looks like: HIGH PRIORITY · Twilight escalation · Data Loss · Alex

The email goes to ben_prod_twilight@centific.com.

4. Admin steps · Incident Report

Sign in as an Admin (or a role that can open Performance).

Open Incident Report

Use either path:

  1. Red pill: tap the red Incident Report pill in the admin bar.
  2. Tabs: tap Performance → tap Incident Report (next to Sessions).

You should see tiles for each report, with the same four icons.

Filter the list

  1. Tap a status tile at the top: All, Police Report, Data Loss, Questions, or Misconduct.
  2. Use the date range the same way you do on Sessions, if you only want a window of days.
  3. Type in the search box to find a name, team, or word from the comment.

Read one report

  1. Tap a tile (or tap its Incident Report pill) to expand it.
  2. You will see type, Environment or Teammate (if misconduct), who sent it, team, session date, time, assignment, email status, the comment, and the Teams reminder when it applies.

Export to Excel

  1. Set the filters you want (the export uses what is on screen).
  2. Tap the export button on the Incident Report page.
  3. Open the downloaded Excel file.
New reports also show on the device that sent them right away. Shared reports from Excel appear for every admin after the PanicLog read flow is connected. Until that URL is in the app, other computers may only show reports sent on that same computer.

5. Who does what

Situation Moderator Admin
Routine setup problem Red shield → Troubleshooting. Or handle it yourself. No action unless asked.
Need managers in chat Red shield → Comm. Then type in Teams. Watch the Teams chat.
Police / danger Call 911 if needed. Then Emergency → Escalate immediately → Contact Police → Call 911. Also tell Ritu and Sander. Open the high-priority email and Incident Report. Follow up the same night.
Lost or bad capture Emergency → Escalate immediately → Data Loss → comment → Send report. Then tell managers in Teams. Open Incident Report → Data Loss. Confirm the file and next steps.
Consent / NDA question you cannot answer Emergency → Escalate immediately → NDA/Consent Question → comment → Send report. Then tell managers in Teams. Open Incident Report → Questions. Reply to the team.
Misconduct, harm, or threat Emergency → Escalate immediately → Misconduct Report → Environment or Teammate → comment → Send report. Tell Ritu and Sander. Open Incident Report → Misconduct. Treat as high priority.
